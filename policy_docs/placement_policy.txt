PLACEMENT ELIGIBILITY & POLICY DOCUMENT
Training & Placement Cell — Academic Year 2026-27

1. GENERAL ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
1.1 A student must have a minimum aggregate CGPA of 6.0 (out of 10) up to the latest completed
    semester to be eligible for campus placement drives.
1.2 A student must not have more than 1 active (standing) backlog at the time of the placement
    drive. Students with 2 or more active backlogs will not be permitted to sit for any company
    drive until backlogs are cleared.
1.3 Minimum attendance of 75% in the current academic year is required to be eligible.
1.4 There is no restriction based on branch/department; all final-year students from all
    departments are eligible unless a specific company drive states otherwise.

2. NUMBER OF OFFERS / "ONE STUDENT ONE OFFER" RULE
2.1 Once a student accepts an offer (Day 1 or Day 2 core companies, package above 6 LPA), the
    student is moved to the "placed" category and is not permitted to sit for further drives,
    except for "dream companies" with package above 15 LPA, which require prior written approval
    from the Placement Officer.
2.2 Students who accept an internship-only or stipend-based offer are still allowed to sit for
    full-time drives.

3. DOCUMENTATION REQUIRED
3.1 Updated resume verified by the department placement coordinator.
3.2 No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the department Head of Department (HOD).
3.3 Valid government photo ID and college ID card.
3.4 Semester-wise mark sheets up to the latest completed semester.

4. DRESS CODE AND CONDUCT
4.1 Formal attire is mandatory for all placement drives, tests, and interviews.
4.2 Any form of misconduct (impersonation, sharing test material, disrespect to recruiters) will
    lead to a debarment of up to 2 years from all placement activities, decided by a disciplinary
    committee.

5. DEBARMENT RULES
5.1 A student who accepts an offer and later backs out without valid, pre-approved reason will be
    debarred from placement activities for the remainder of the academic year.
5.2 A student who does not appear for a confirmed interview slot without prior intimation
    (minimum 12 hours notice) will receive a written warning; a second instance leads to a
    1-semester debarment.
5.3 Absence from more than 3 scheduled pre-placement talks (PPTs) in a semester without valid
    reason may lead to a temporary suspension of eligibility for that semester's drives.

6. RE-ATTEMPT / BACKLOG CLEARANCE POLICY
6.1 A student who clears all active backlogs before a specific drive's eligibility cut-off date
    (announced separately for each company) becomes eligible for that drive, subject to meeting
    the CGPA criterion.
6.2 Backlog clearance must be reflected in official academic records (grade card / transcript)
    before the cut-off date; provisional pass certificates are not accepted for this purpose.

7. HIGHER STUDIES / OFF-CAMPUS OPPORTUNITIES
7.1 Students opting for higher studies (GATE, GRE, CAT, etc.) may still register for on-campus
    placements but must clearly indicate their preference during registration.
7.2 The college does not restrict students from applying to off-campus opportunities
    independently, but students who accept an off-campus core offer above 6 LPA are also moved to
    the "placed" category as per Section 2.

8. GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL
8.1 Any grievance regarding eligibility, test conduct, or company shortlisting must be submitted
    in writing to the Placement Officer within 48 hours of the concerned event.
8.2 The Placement Committee's decision on any grievance is final.

NOTE: This document does not cover stipend structures, specific company package details, or
      interview question patterns for individual companies — those are shared separately by the
      Placement Cell before each drive.
